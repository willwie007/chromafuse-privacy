Chromafuse is published by **Shanghai Dragon Pixel Science and Technology Consulting Company Limited**. If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at: **contact@dragonpixelstudio.com**.

## 2. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use the App and which services are enabled, we or our service providers may collect the following categories of information:

- **Device and technical information**, such as device model, operating system, app version, language, country or region, IP address, crash logs, and performance diagnostics.
- **Advertising identifiers**, including the Android Advertising ID, which may be used by advertising partners to provide and measure ads.
- **Gameplay and usage data**, such as session length, feature usage, level progress, interactions, reward claims, and general analytics events.
- **Purchase information**, such as confirmation that an in-app purchase was made. We do not receive your full payment card details; payments are processed by Google Play or other authorized payment providers.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use information for the following purposes:

- To operate, maintain, and improve Chromafuse.
- To support gameplay features such as progress, achievements, leaderboards, and game-related services.
- To process and verify in-app purchases.
- To show advertisements and measure ad performance.
- To understand app performance, usage trends, crashes, and bugs.
- To prevent fraud, abuse, unauthorized activity, and technical issues.
- To comply with legal obligations.

We may use analytics and diagnostic tools to understand how users interact with Chromafuse, improve stability, fix bugs, and improve gameplay balance and product decisions. Analytics data is generally used in aggregated or pseudonymous form where possible.
